PHListing — write the listing, launch through Product Hunt's official API.

What it does

  • Writes the whole listing — tagline, description, launch tags, categories, pricing type and the maker's first comment, all inside Product Hunt's own character limits.
  • Uses real tags and categories — names are checked against Product Hunt's own lists, so an invented tag is corrected rather than dropped in silence at submission time.
  • Submits through Product Hunt's API — no form filling. Your images are uploaded and the draft is created in under a minute.
  • Nothing goes live without you — every run ends in a private draft or a scheduled launch, and it can stop before the final step so you press the button yourself.

What you need

  • A Product Hunt account — you approve PHListing on Product Hunt's own page.
  • An AI provider of your own, or a local model. PHListing includes no AI and resells none.
  • Google Chrome 116 or newer.

PHListing is free. There is no PHListing account and no subscription.

Your data

Everything you create stays in your browser. There is no PHListing server and no analytics. Only two things leave: what you ask to have written goes to the AI provider you chose, and your finished listing goes to Product Hunt.
